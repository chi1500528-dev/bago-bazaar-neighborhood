# Full-image hero banner (home page)

Replace the current split hero (mobile compact card + desktop text/collage) with one full-bleed photo card.

## Design
- One new high-quality photo of an authentic Myanmar market (fresh vegetables, fruit, local groceries), with a calmer area on the lower-left for text.
- Photo fills the whole rounded card, `object-cover`, positioned to keep produce visible.
- Forest-green gradient overlay, strongest bottom-left, fading to clear top-right.
- Text bottom-left: small gold eyebrow "The local market of Bago Region", ivory headline "Every shop in Bago, every product you need", ivory/cream "Start shopping" button (forest-green text) — same link as today. Myanmar/English via existing dictionary.
- Removes the "How it works" strip, dot pattern, pagoda watermark and collage from the hero (they were the "empty side" fixes).

## Sizing
- Mobile: full width, ~4:3.5 aspect (about 300–340px tall), headline ~1.5–1.75rem, button 44px tall.
- Desktop: ~21:8, max ~420px tall, headline ~2.75rem, text column max ~560px.
- No animations added.

## Not changed
Header, search, categories, nav, product/shop lists, data, sign-in.
